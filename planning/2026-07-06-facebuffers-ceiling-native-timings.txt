  HEADLINE: facebuffers takes Safari 26-27 → 40-44 fps at matched CPU (+~68%).
  The interval improvement (31 → ~19 ms) splits ~evenly: render issuance
  7.4 → 1.4 ms AND post-callback slack 17 → 11 ms. The slack DROP is the new
  finding — it means Safari's slack is largely DEFERRED PER-CANVAS RASTER of
  the issued draw ops, reducible by cutting op count (which buffering does and
  onecanvas didn't). Overturns the timings-9/10 "slack = composite-area" read.
  CAVEAT: this is the OPTIMISTIC ceiling — achieved by letting all 16 faces
  step at tick-rate (fidelity violation). Fidelity-preserving fraction is
  still unmeasured (needs middle-share instrumentation). Memory: face buffers
  70.1 MB → TOTAL 328 MB at 4K.
