unzip,
serve on a fresh port,
open all.html?lat=…&lon=…&tickprofile=1&noprobe=1
wait ~2s for the refresh sampler,
hold +day ~6s, release, paste the console block.

Three runs for medians, one probe-on run as cross-check, ideally in both Safari and Chrome.

The slack line is the headline number: it tells us how much real browser-side back-pressure exists per frame, which is the H2 question the whole Phase 1 branch hangs on.
