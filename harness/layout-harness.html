Transitions

When on, the active anchor is chosen from the current viewport aspect (W/H) — drag Width/Height to scrub across the transition thresholds below. Each threshold defaults to the log midpoint √(Aₙ·Aₙ₊₁) between adjacent anchors; the slider moves where the switch happens.

Footer chrome

Single footer row. The noon toggle is a center icon; tapping it raises the Midnight/Noon pill as an on-demand overlay that may overlap the dial (rare), so it costs the layout nothing. The viewport is the safe/design rect.

Header row

A reserved top band; the layout is computed in the remaining height and shifted down (notch ignored for now).

Candidate rules

Outer dials keep their distance to the main dial and their side margin, repositioning angularly. Main Δy nudges the main dial vertically (− = up). Date sits midway between the map's bottom and the dial's top.
A4 landscape: distribute alt & az evenly (equal gaps) in the left column between the moon's bottom and the weekday's top, then give ecl/eot the same y's. Trades a larger ecl↔map gap for top/bottom symmetry.
A5 iPhone landscape (fully computed). Main dial fills 0.95·H (overlaps header+footer). Margins (outer dials + map right) halved; moon/map sit that same gap below the header. Map grows until its lower-left corner's radial gap = the margin. Outer-dial radius solved so eot's margin, eot↔ecl, and ecl↔main (radial) all equal the margin; alt/az reuse it. Left/right pairs centre in moon↔date / map↔date independently. Date drops so the deepest weekday descender (Sun–Sat) sits the margin above the footer; weekday & date2 share that baseline, A4 sizes, no dots. Moon grows until its radial gap to alt/az, or the alt/az-bottom↔weekday-top gap, hits the margin (also clamped to the left margin / main-dial rim); centred over alt/az.
A6 outer-circle size (moon + alt/az/ecl/eot) as a fraction of the main-dial radius. Main dial keeps ⌀ = row height; at 0.65 the outer dials are smaller than the dial (reproduces the earlier look). Overflow is still absorbed by the gap rule.
A6 primary font ("Friday", "Jun 19") as a fraction of the outer-dial diameter (so it tracks the outer-dial size at every sample). 2026/PDT stay proportional to it.
Asq (square) map width as a fraction of the window width (map is 2:1, so mapH = ½·mapW). The main dial then fills the remaining height with a full gap (2·halfPad) above (to the map), below (to the footer top), and the map keeps the same gap to the header bottom.
Asq outer-dial position: fraction along the radial from the main-dial rim to the footer's top-left corner where the az dial sits (0 = on the rim, 1 = at the corner, 0.5 = midway). eot mirrors az across the vertical centreline; alt/ecl mirror az/eot across the dial's horizontal centreline. All at the inner sub-dial size.
Awide moon size as a fraction of its computed maximum (largest circle clearing the left edge, the alt/az column with a full gap, and the date block below with a full gap). 1 = the max rule.
Awide map size as a fraction of its computed maximum (largest 2:1 rect clearing the right edge, header/footer, and the ecl/eot column with a full gap). 1 = the max rule.
